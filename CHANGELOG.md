## Firefight: World War III - the Final War™ 2.3.0 (2640)

**Version:** 2.3.0 Release

**Version Code:** 2030007

**Update Type:** Major Content Update

**Update Contents:**

- **Added** the `American-HSTV-L` vehicle;
- **Added** Merkava `Mk.3B`, `Mk.3C`, `Mk.3D`, `Mk.4 LIC`, `Mk.4B`, `Mk.4M` (In Production), with all-new hull/turret images for the Merkava family (Mk.1 ~ Mk.4M);
- **Added** weapons `WEAPON_CANNON_125_ZPT98`, `WEAPON_CANNON_105_SHARIR` series, `WEAPON_CANNON_120_MG251` series, `WEAPON_CANNON_75_XM274`;
- **Added** the Israeli Mechanized Infantry Section, Sayeret Reconnaissance Team and Reserve Infantry Section (based on active IDF references), the Nahal Brigade section leader now carries a Desert Eagle;
- **Remade** the US Marine Corps: new Marine Rifle Squad / Marine Rifle Squad (LAV) / Marine Weapons Squad;
- **Added** polished Chooser button images (custom/sound/networking).
- **Improved** data of the `M1A1AIM`, `M1A2` series (TUSK/SEP V2~V4), `Leopard 2A4`, `M1128` and other vehicles;
- **Improved** data and naming of the `M242`, `MK44`, `M61`, `M320/M320A1`, `AT4`, `FGM-148`, `SSG 69`, `FN MAG`, `M2HB` and other weapons;
- **Improved** all unit comments rewritten (240 units).
- **Fixed** data tag errors in `WEAPON_CANNON_25_KBA_B02`, `Russian-Mortar 2B11`, `Russian-BTR-D`;
- **Fixed** about 50 missing sound references;
- **Fixed** 27 non-standard weapon file names, 20+ spelling errors and 2 mojibake cases;
- **Changed** vehicle image file names fully standardized (396 files aligned with the vanilla convention), Merkava unit IDs and image names unified (`Mk.1` ~ `Mk.4 LIC`);
- **Changed** unit file name prefixes (`French-AMX56`, `Russian-T-90M Bophna`, etc.), long names stripped of stacked generic words, 10 short-name collisions differentiated;
- **Changed** infantry section comments no longer list grenade counts (vanilla convention), section comments completed with training quality sentences;
- **Changed** the Israeli faction marked as `In Production`, third-party assets of *Firefight: Gaza Ground Warfare* registered under the MIT license;
- **Changed** version numbering upgraded to the 7-digit scheme (BMMSSNN), data file indentation standardized (tabs).
- Fixed other bugs.
